EBITDA means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. It is often used as a proxy for core operating performance, but it is not cash flow.

Free cash flow (FCF) is commonly defined as operating cash flow minus capital expenditures. Positive and growing FCF usually signals improving financial flexibility.

Price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio equals market price per share divided by earnings per share. A high P/E can indicate growth expectations, but sector comparisons are necessary.

Debt-to-equity ratio compares total debt to shareholders' equity. Higher leverage can increase return potential and financial risk.

Net interest margin (NIM) is a key bank metric measuring net interest income relative to earning assets.
